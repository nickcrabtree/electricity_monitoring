## Grafana dashboards (state as of 2026-09-25)

The dashboards live only in Grafana's database on ubuntu64 (`192.168.86.123:3000`), not in git, so changes are recorded here. Edit them through the HTTP API with the `GRAFANA_TOKEN` in `secrets_local.env` (POST `/api/dashboards/db` with the fetched `version` and `overwrite: false`, so a concurrent edit is refused rather than clobbered); Grafana keeps every prior version under `/api/dashboards/uid/<uid>/versions/`. The graphite-web render API used for ad-hoc queries is on port **81** of the same host.

- **"Electricity Monitoring v2"** (uid `c1df4faa-bfe6-41a9-ae23-2f55a3a7f97b`) is the maintained one; created by Claude Code 2026-09-04, now version 4 (2026-09-27). Panels: Usage, per-device daily/weekly/monthly kWh, Aggregate Whole House, and **Ovens** (Main Oven and Top Oven `power_watts`, added 2026-09-25). Every panel uses wildcard targets (`home.electricity.*.*.power_watts`, `home.electricity.tuya.*.*kwh*daily`, ...) so a newly discovered device appears without a dashboard edit. The Usage panel's target is `aliasByNode(home.electricity.*.*.power_watts, 2, 3)`, so legend entries read `tuya.main_oven` rather than the full path. **Do not wrap it in `removeEmptySeries`**: version 3 did, which tidied away the six devices with no data (Kasa fridge, Kasa tumble dryer, dishwasher, freezer, hair straighteners, multifunction alarm) and thereby hid an outage; Nick reverted that on 2026-09-27. A dead device should show as an empty legend row. The `meter.power_watts` series sits on the right axis via a `byName` override (`meter.power_watts` after aliasing).
- **"Electricity Monitoring"** (uid `l_kJLKgvz`) is the 2025 original with legacy `graph` panels, last edited by admin 2025-12-29. Left as is.
- No other dashboard references electricity metrics.

## House power topology (recorded 2026-09-25, during an Equiwatt event)

What is behind which plug, and what each switch actually controls. Established by reading live power per plug, the HA entity list, and process lists on the hosts; inferences are marked as such. Machine-level facts (where hosts live, how to reach them) are in `~/code/AGENTS.md`.

- **"Shed" plug (`bfca739a0f4da457a45his`, Tuya, main LAN) is the master feed for the whole shed**, at the house end of the shed's single power cable. Switching it off cuts the shed freezer, calcite (which hosts the Grafana/Carbon `ubuntu64` VM and the Home Assistant VM), blackpi2 (the coolbox controller and this repo's main-LAN pollers) and the shed Nest node. Never switch it off to save power. Its ~150 W is dominated by the shed freezer, which from 2026-10-27 has its own Tuya plug (`Shed Freezer 2026-10-27`, `bf0a2cd8043ff422e6tnxt`, 192.168.86.71, metric `home.electricity.tuya.shed_freezer_2026_10_27`) and can therefore be switched separately.
- The **Amaryllis fridge** is the second-largest shed load (~70 W when running) and is the only shed load that can be dropped for a couple of hours. It is under blackpi2's tight control loop (`temp_humid_graphite_panicboard.py`, root cron every 2 min), which the Equiwatt poller suspends via `input_boolean.amaryllis_fridge_disabled` for the duration of an event (see `~/code/AGENTS.md`, "Equiwatt power hold").
- **quartz is not on the Office shelves plug.** `switch.office_shelves_socket_1` (`10105863c4dd57078c2e`, ~11 W) was misleadingly named `switch.quartz_server_socket_1` in Home Assistant until 2026-09-27, when it was renamed and the one automation that referenced it ("Reload Tuya when the integration is dead") was updated. quartz is almost certainly the **"N desk"** plug (`10105863c4dd57021c2e`): ~175 W with the GPU idle at 46 W and the 20-core host at load 0.2, rising with GPU work. That is an inference from the power figures, not a traced cable.
- **Both ovens are Tuya "dlq" metering circuit breakers on the OpenWrt subnet**, reachable only from flint: Main Oven `bf52baba2305f136datzwi` at 192.168.1.157, Top Oven `bf64fd8c51015ede6db23j` at 192.168.1.135 (protocol 3.4, DHCP addresses). They never appear in a broadcast scan from the main LAN, which is why `--discover` now scans via flint. Real figures: Main Oven preheats at ~3.7 kW / 15.5 A then cycles ~1.5 kW; Top Oven ~3.2 kW / 13.3 A. In Home Assistant they are `switch.top_oven_switch` / `sensor.top_oven_total_energy` and `switch.main_oven_switch` / `sensor.main_oven_total_energy` (renamed 2026-09-27 from the untranslated product name `ji_liang_duan_lu_qi` / `_2`); HA exposes no power sensor for them and its total-energy sensor flickers between 0.001 and 0.002 kWh, so **do not use HA for the ovens**. The Top Oven's HA entities were `unavailable` from mid-September until a Tuya config-entry reload on 2026-09-27 (the entry itself was `loaded`, so the failed-entry reload script did nothing; a plain reload fixed it); flint's local polling was unaffected throughout. The **Towel Rail** (`bffbd6e8894a52ef8e6qba`) is also on the OpenWrt subnet.
- **Aggregate de-duplication.** `aggregate_energy.py` reads `aggregate_hierarchy.json` (parent device key -> child device keys, keyed `tuya.<format_device_name(friendly name)>`). A child is left out of `home.electricity.aggregate.power_watts` whenever its parent is in that cycle's data, because the parent's reading already contains it; if the parent is missing the children are summed instead (logged once per transition). Per-device metrics are unchanged. Configured: parent `tuya.shed` with children Amaryllis fridge, Amaryllis heater, Shed Freezer 2026-10-27, Quartz server shed (`tuya.quartz_server_shed`, `bfec5b5d459e565242hhz9`, 192.168.86.70; quartz's own plug since it moved into the shed on 2026-10-28; never switch it off), Shed weather esp32 (not yet monitored), `tuya.electric_blanket` (device `bf11db5eb189bb21e7uscg`, the cloud's "Small water butt pump") and Big water butt pump (HA bridge, no power sensor so never in the sum anyway). Nick confirmed on 2026-10-27 that all six sit behind the Shed plug. The aggregate steps down on the deploy date; older data stays double-counted. Flint does not run the aggregator.
- **Unknown Tuya device `bf0ea1e2ab51599d86ckjp` at 192.168.1.155** (protocol 3.4), seen by flint's scan; not in `devices.json` or the 21-device Tuya cloud list. Identity not yet established.
- **Kasa fridge and Kasa tumble dryer produce no data** (nothing in Graphite for the last 7 days as of 2026-09-25); the HA `switch.tumble_dryer` entity is a restored, unavailable ghost. The Kasa poller on blackpi2 is running, so this is device-side or network-side, not yet investigated.
- **Whole-house meter: an ESP32 in the garage reading the electricity meter's pulse LED.** Canonical firmware is **`~/scripts/electricity_monitor/`** in the `scripts` repo (MicroPython 1.23; `main.py`, `monitor.py`, `config.py`, `pulse_test.py`, `upload*.sh`, `LM393-migration-plan.md`); the copy in `~/code/home_assistant/imported_from_scripts/electricity_monitor/` is a partial 2026-05-15 import (no `main.py`, `pulse_test.py` or upload scripts) whose `monitor.py` was later tidied and **never flashed**. History: bare LDR on the ADC until 2025-11-09, then migrated to an **LM393 light-comparator module on GPIO34** (falling-edge interrupt per flash, module has its own pull-up, threshold set by the pot on the module), 4000 imp/kWh, Wi-Fi `12 Lloyd`; last known upload 2025-11-10 over WebREPL, so whether the 2025-11-25 NTP changes are on the board is unrecorded. Board is **`mpy-esp32.local`** (DHCP; 192.168.86.11 on 2026-09-26), WebREPL on :8266 started at boot (password is in the tracked upload script), no USB cable needed; on-board `/state.json` persists the pulse count. It publishes to **both** sinks: Graphite `home.electricity.meter.{power_watts,power_kw,total_kwh,pulse_count}` every 10 s (the `meter` series on the Grafana Usage and Whole House panels) and HA `sensor.electricity_consumption` / `sensor.electricity_power` every 5 min. Nothing on the Mac, quartz or blackpi2 schedules it; it is autonomous. **Status 2026-09-26: alive but blind.** It answers ping, still reports every minute to Graphite and every 5 min to HA, so Wi-Fi and firmware are up; but `power_watts` has read 0 since 17:12 on 2026-09-24 and `pulse_count` last advanced at 13:46 on 2026-09-25 (one stray pulse), then 15,479,500 flat; `total_kwh` stuck at 3869.875. A stuck-high LM393 DO (pot drifted, module mis-aimed at the meter's very faint LED, or a module fault) gives exactly this pattern. Nick's to-do: confirm via WebREPL (`stats()`) and the module's LED, re-aim/re-trim, or find a better source of whole-house data. Until then `sensor.electricity_power` (0 W) is **not** a usable house total, and `home.electricity.aggregate.power_watts` is the sum of monitored plugs only (~340 W with quartz idle, ~480 W with quartz training).
